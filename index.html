How I approach analysis

The same four steps I used to run QA, applied to business problems. Each one comes from work I have already done.

  1. 1

    Understand the need

    Ask what the business is trying to achieve and what “good” looks like before touching any data.

    From: setting quality standards across projects
  2. 2

    Analyse the data

    Pull the numbers together, track the right metrics and look for patterns and gaps.

    From: QA performance tracking in Google Sheets
  3. 3

    Document clearly

    Write guidelines and reports that a team can follow without needing me in the room.

    From: improving annotation guidelines
  4. 4

    Check and improve

    Review the outcome, give feedback and keep refining the process.

    From: leading and mentoring the QA team
